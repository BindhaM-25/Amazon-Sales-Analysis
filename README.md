# 📊 AMAZON PRODUCT SALES ANALYSIS

### Management Dashboard – Business Insight & Performance Overview

---

## 🎯 1. Executive Summary

The *Amazon Product Sales Analysis Dashboard* provides a consolidated view of product sales performance, sales trends, product-category contribution, and customer review activity.

The dashboard is designed to help management quickly understand:

* 💰 Overall Sales Performance
* 📈 Monthly & Weekly Sales Trends
* 🛍️ Product Category Contribution
* 🏆 Top Performing Products
* ⭐ Customer Review Performance
* 📊 YTD and QTD Business Performance

This dashboard enables management to identify *high-performing products, sales growth patterns, category opportunities, and areas requiring business attention.*

---

# 💰 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 💵 YTD Sales – $2.2M

*Year-to-Date Sales* represents the total sales generated from the beginning of the year up to the selected reporting period.

*Business Insight:*
A strong YTD sales value indicates healthy overall revenue generation. Management can use this KPI to monitor progress against annual sales targets and evaluate whether the business is on track for expected growth.

---

### 📅 QTD Sales – $811K

*Quarter-to-Date Sales* measures sales generated during the current quarter.

*Business Insight:*
This KPI helps management evaluate short-term performance and compare the current quarter against previous quarters. It can also support timely corrective actions when quarterly sales performance is below expectations.

---

### 🛒 YTD Products Sold – 28K

This KPI represents the total number of products sold during the year-to-date period.

*Business Insight:*
The volume of products sold provides an indication of customer demand and purchasing activity. Management can compare sales value with product volume to understand changes in product mix and average sales contribution.

---

### ⭐ YTD Reviews – 19M

This KPI highlights the overall customer review activity associated with the products.

*Business Insight:*
Customer reviews provide an important indication of customer engagement and product visibility. Higher review activity can support product credibility, customer confidence, and future purchasing decisions.

---

# 📈 3. Sales by Month

### 📊 Visual: Monthly Sales Trend

The *Sales by Month* chart presents the movement of sales across different months.

*Key Business Perspective:*

* 📈 Helps identify months with strong sales performance.
* 🔍 Highlights periods of sales growth or slowdown.
* 🗓️ Supports seasonal demand analysis.
* 🎯 Helps management plan sales targets for upcoming months.
* 📦 Can support inventory and procurement planning based on demand patterns.

*Management Interpretation:*
The upward movement visible toward the later months indicates an improvement in sales performance. Management can investigate the reasons behind this increase, such as promotional campaigns, seasonal demand, product launches, or increased customer activity.

---

# 📅 4. Sales by Week

### 📊 Visual: Weekly Sales Performance

The *Sales by Week* chart provides a more detailed view of sales fluctuations across individual weeks.

*Key Business Perspective:*

* 📌 Identifies high-performing weeks.
* 📉 Highlights sudden drops in sales.
* 📈 Tracks short-term sales momentum.
* 🎯 Helps evaluate campaign and promotional effectiveness.
* 📦 Supports weekly inventory and supply planning.

*Management Interpretation:*
Weekly analysis allows management to move beyond monthly trends and identify specific periods where customer demand increases or decreases.

This can help the business respond quickly through *pricing, promotions, inventory allocation, and marketing activities.*

---

# 🛍️ 5. Product Category Performance

### 📊 Visual: Product Category Performance Table

The category table compares products based on:

* 💰 YTD Sales
* 📅 QTD Sales
* 📊 % Contribution to YTD Sales

*Business Perspective:*

The table helps management understand which product categories are contributing the highest share of revenue.

### 🏆 High-performing categories

Categories with higher YTD sales and higher percentage contribution should receive continued attention in:

* Inventory availability
* Marketing investment
* Promotional campaigns
* Pricing strategy
* Customer retention

### ⚠️ Lower-performing categories

Lower-contributing categories should be reviewed to identify:

* Weak customer demand
* Pricing issues
* Low product visibility
* High competition
* Product quality concerns
* Insufficient marketing

*Management Action:*
Management can use this comparison to prioritize resources toward categories with the highest business potential while developing improvement strategies for weaker categories.
